# Third-party notices - Oblivion MO2 Theme

No third-party files are included.

## Apocrypha Menu Framework, Njordlinger MO2 Theme

The frame and background art (`src\amf-art\`) is Apocrypha Menu Framework for Oblivion Remastered's - original art,
drawn from shapes by that repo's `tools\make_mapedge_frame.py`, no game file - and the icons (`src\icons\`) are the
Njordlinger MO2 Theme's, recoloured. Both are by the same author (ApocryphaRealm, GPL-3.0-or-later).

## Mod Organizer 2

Widget selectors and the toolbar action names come from Mod Organizer 2 (https://github.com/ModOrganizer2/modorganizer, GPL-3.0).
